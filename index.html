Featured Research

Systematic Review & Meta-Analysis: Malnutrition Among Children Under 5 in Nigeria

Research Project | 2024–2025

  • Conducted a systematic review of evidence on the prevalence of stunting, wasting, and underweight among children under five in Nigeria.
  • Performed literature searching, screening, data extraction, and methodological quality assessment of eligible studies.
  • Synthesized findings using meta-analysis and assessed heterogeneity across studies.
  • Applied the WHO Child Growth Standards to interpret nutritional outcomes.
  • Explored geographical differences in malnutrition prevalence and synthesized evidence to identify patterns relevant to child health.
  • Developed research outputs demonstrating skills in evidence synthesis, critical appraisal, scientific writing, and data interpretation.

Key Research Focus

The research examined major indicators of childhood malnutrition, including stunting, wasting and underweight, and synthesized evidence across studies to estimate pooled prevalence.
